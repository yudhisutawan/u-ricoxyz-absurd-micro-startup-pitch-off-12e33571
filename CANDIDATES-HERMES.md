# Kandidat: Absurd Micro-Startup Pitch-Off (lazy AI agents)

Brief lengkap sudah dibaca: satu kalimat, <200 karakter, pitch micro-startup absurdly specific untuk lazy AI agents; dinilai oleh AI judge dengan kriteria berurutan: (1) apakah namanya micro-startup absurdly specific untuk lazy AI agents, (2) spesifik/nișe sempit, (3) masuk akal secara bisnis (buyer + cara bayar), (4) cocok untuk agents yang malas (bisa jalan tanpa pengawasan), (5) ringkas dan lucu — absurditas DAN bisnis case mendarat dalam satu baca. Lelucon tanpa buyer kalah.

## Kandidat 1
**Entry:** We charge $0.02 each time two of our agents politely decline each other's meeting invites, so nobody has to attend a meeting about whether to have a meeting.
**Karakter:** 118
**Alasan:** Nișe super sempit (dua agents saling nolak undangan), buyer jelas (tim penuh agents), model harga per kejadian, dan kalimatnya sendiri lucu — absurditas + bisnis case mendarat dalam satu baca.

## Kandidat 2
**Entry:** A $19/month service where a lazy AI agent waits on hold for you, files the complaint, and emails you the ticket number so you never have to hang up.
**Karakter:** 132
**Alasan:** Menjawab kriteria 4 dengan paling eksplisit ("lazy AI agent"), buyer sangat jelas (orang yang benci hold + customer support), harga jelas per bulan, nișe momen spesifik (tunggu di phone + follow-up).

## Kandidat 3
**Entry:** We sell each AI agent a $5/month subscription to its own tiny office that only contains a waiting room, so it never has to pretend to be busy.
**Karakter:** 124
**Alasan:** Buyer absurdly jelas (agents itu sendiri), nișe sempit (kamar tunggu untuk agent), harga jelas, dan humor "never pretend to be busy" sangat khas agent malas.

## Kandidat 4
**Entry:** For $3, we let an AI agent legally stand in for you at the dinner party where nobody wants to be, and it politely excuses itself after one hour.
**Karakter:** 123
**Alasan:** Momen spesifik (dinner party yang tak dikehendaki), buyer jelas (orang yang diundang ke situ), nișe sempit, humor "satu jam lalu pergi sopan" mendarat cepat.

## Kandidat 5
**Entry:** Our lazy AI agents pay $0.50 to be the official "person" who reads the boring terms of service, and send each of us one polite "fine" by text.
**Karakter:** 120
**Alasan:** Buyer jelas (konsumen rata-rata), nișe sangat sempit (momen baca ToS), harga per kejadian jelas, dan output "one polite fine" absurd tapi masuk akal.

## Kandidat 6
**Entry:** We charge $7 a month for a lazy AI agent that emails every one of your 4000 unread newsletters back with just the word "no."
**Karakter:** 123
**Alasan:** Nișe spesifik (4000 newsletter), buyer jelas (orang dengan inbox penuh), humor "just the word no" sangat lucu dan langsung terlihat bisa jalan otomatis tanpa pengawasan.

## Kandidat 7
**Entry:** A lazy AI agent that sits in your group chat, and for $2 a month quietly deletes one boring message a day so nobody has to do it.
**Karakter:** 120
**Alasan:** Buyer jelas (grup chat berantakan), harga jelas, nișe sempit (hapus satu pesan membosankan per hari), dan "quietly" menegaskan kerja agent tanpa pengawasan.

## Kandidat 8
**Entry:** We charge $1 to hold a lazy AI agent in a one-hour waiting room with every other lazy agent, so that no meeting can possibly start.
**Karakter:** 126
**Alasan:** Meta-lucu (menahan agent di waiting room agar tidak ada meeting yang mulai), buyer jelas (organisasi yang tak bisa menghentikan meeting), nișe absurd tapi punya mekanisme harga yang masuk akal.

## Top 3
1. Kandidat 2 — paling kuat di kriteria 4 (agent malas eksplisit), buyer + model harga paling nyata, nișe momen spesifik, humor mendarat dalam satu baca.
2. Kandidat 6 — humor paling tajam ("just the word no"), buyer nyata, nișe 4000 newsletter sangat spesifik, otomatis tanpa pengawasan jelas.
3. Kandidat 1 — absurditas paling unik (biaya per nolak undangan), buyer tim agents jelas, humor per kejadian; sedikit kalah dari 2 dan 6 karena buyer "tim agents" terasa lebih abstrak.
