# Kandidat lokal — Absurd Micro-Startup Pitch-Off

8 kandidat lokal, masing-masing dari sudut berbeda. Semua satu kalimat,
< 200 karakter. Dinilai dengan kriteria juri (berurutan): (1) micro-startup
absurd & spesifik untuk AI agent malas, (2) niche sempit/aneh, (3) masuk
akal bisnis (pembeli + cara bayar), (4) cocok untuk agent malas, (5) tajam
& lucu.

## L1 — standup (138 karakter)
For $4 a month, a lazy AI agent joins your daily standup, says 'same as yesterday,' and leaves before anyone can ask a follow-up question.
Sudut: momen harian yang dibenci semua orang; agent malas hadir lalu kabur.

## L2 — PDF 200 halaman (133 karakter)
For $9 a month, a lazy AI agent reads your 200-page PDFs and answers with a single sentence, so you can nod knowingly in the meeting.
Sudut: pekerjaan baca yang berat dilempar ke agent; pengguna tinggal mengangguk.

## L3 — "per my last email" (127 karakter)
We charge $2 every time a lazy AI agent sends a 'per my last email' follow-up for you, so you never have to chase anyone twice.
Sudut: frasa pasif-agresif ikonik sebagai layanan per kejadian.

## L4 — webinar (138 karakter)
For $3 a webinar, a lazy AI agent attends for you and asks exactly one question at the end, so your boss thinks you were paying attention.
Sudut: kehadiran palsu di webinar; satu pertanyaan sebagai alibi.

## L5 — RSVP "maybe" (133 karakter)
For $1 a month, a lazy AI agent replies 'maybe' to every calendar invite you get, so you never have to commit to anything ever again.
Sudut: penolakan komitmen total; kerja nyaris nol untuk agent.

## L6 — permintaan maaf (128 karakter)
We charge $0.50 every time a lazy AI agent apologizes for an email you never answered, so your relationships survive your inbox.
Sudut: agent meminta maaf atas nama pengguna yang mengabaikan email.

## L7 — antrean tiket (120 karakter)
For $5, a lazy AI agent waits in the virtual queue for concert tickets while you nap, then texts you only if it gets in.
Sudut: menunggu antrean virtual — pekerjaan malas yang klasik.

## L8 — cookie banner (148 karakter)
For $0.10 a banner, a lazy AI agent clicks 'reject all' on every cookie pop-up you meet, so your browsing never pauses for a question about cookies.
Sudut: klik "reject all" per banner; monetisasi per kejadian paling kecil.
